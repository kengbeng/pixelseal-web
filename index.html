      About PixelSeal

      Files stay in this browser. No uploads, analytics or external assets. Export checks marked areas for remaining text and black fill; it is not a complete document security audit. Review the exported document before sharing.

      Interactive forms are not supported. OCR supports English and simplified Chinese scans; accuracy depends on scan quality. OCR is used for finding redactions and does not add a searchable text layer to exported files. Very large PDFs may exceed browser memory. Original files are never overwritten.
